Hello, I'm Brett. I am a software developer living in Philadelphia.

Professional experience

Since 2022, I have been a Senior Software Engineer for the City of Philadelphia, leading backend development on the following two web apps:

  1. lobbying.phila.gov: A public database of lobbying activity in Philadelphia in compliace with the City's Lobbying Law and enforced by its Board of Ethics, released September 2026.
  2. public-safety-reports.phila.gov: An online portal for requesting a digital copy of a crash or fire report from Philadelphia's Department of Records, released January 2024.

Prior to working at the City I was a full-stack developer at The Vanguard Group in Malvern, PA.
